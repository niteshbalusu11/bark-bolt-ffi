## Remaining limits

No actual end-user `.noahbackup` archive was supplied. Compatibility is tested
against the initialized schema42 fixture and real snapshot exports; validation
also loads typed Bark/BDK state in a migrated temporary copy. Physical devices
and every minimum OS were not runtime-tested. Terminal pending-round outcomes
have exhaustive Rust conversion tests; hosts execute the empty pending query.
The pinned Bark does not expose unsigned funding round IDs or retain settled
Lightning HTLC/movement details; documented empty/optional fields reflect that.
P2 balance refinements and BOLT11 payment secrets remain deferred.
